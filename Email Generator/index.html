Library Letter Generator

How to use this tool:

  1. Select your team and the appropriate letter template from the dropdowns.
  2. Fill in the required fields to personalize the letter.
  3. Click "Generate Letter" to prepare the text.
  4. Use the copy buttons to quickly grab the subject and body.
  5. Paste into your email client and you're ready to send!
